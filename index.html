01 / About

Research with a path to real-world impact.

I work broadly across NLP and LLM systems, with an occasional deep dive into BioNLP.

I develop broadly applicable methods for retrieval, knowledge grounding, and LLM agents. When a problem calls for domain expertise, I bring those ideas into BioNLP settings such as medical consultation, clinical-trial tracking, and pediatric emergency support.

I am completing a B.S. in Data Science at The Catholic University of Korea and will join Korea University’s Data Mining & Information Systems Lab ↗ through the integrated M.S./Ph.D. program in Computer Science under Prof. Jaewoo Kang.
